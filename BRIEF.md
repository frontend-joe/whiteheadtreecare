# Project Brief — Whitehead Tree Care Website

**From:** Dave Whitehead — Whitehead Tree Care
**To:** Web Designer
**Subject:** New website for our tree surgery business

---

Hi,

Thanks for agreeing to take a look at this. Here's a rundown of what we're after.

## About us

I'm Dave, I run Whitehead Tree Care. We're a family-run tree surgery business based in
Birmingham, covering the Midlands within about 50 miles (Solihull, Coventry, Walsall,
Wolverhampton and so on). I started the business back in 2009 and we've got over 20 years of
experience between us. Our main jobs are **tree surgery, tree felling and stump removal**.
We're fully qualified, insured and take safety seriously.

## The problem

Our current site is looking dated and doesn't really represent how professional we are these
days. It's a bit too jokey and doesn't give people much confidence. I want something that
looks modern and premium and makes people trust us straight away — but it still needs to feel
like a real family business, not a faceless corporate thing.

## What I'd like

- A clean, modern one-page site that scrolls nicely, with smooth animations as you go down the page.
- A proper **video at the top** — something with trees/woodland that looks the part. Big impact
  when you first land on it.
- Keep all the sections we've got now, just done better:
  - What we do (the three services)
  - Why choose us (we're digital/easy to deal with, fully qualified, and usually booked up in advance)
  - A bit about the business and the areas we cover
  - The team — I'd like to put real photos of me, JJ and the crew on there
  - A way to get in touch / request a quote
- I'd love a **testimonials section** with a few reviews from happy customers, ideally something
  a bit interactive that people can swipe through.
- A **"Get a Quote" form** that actually emails me when someone fills it in. Also want my phone,
  email and WhatsApp easy to find.

## Style

Keep it fairly minimal and clean — lots of white space, with **green** as the main colour
(fits the tree theme). Professional but warm. Needs to work perfectly on mobile since most
people will find us on their phones.

## Tone

Confident and trustworthy. Drop the gimmicky stuff from the old site — I want people to read it
and think "these are the people I want doing my trees."

No mad rush, but sooner the better. Give me a shout if you need any photos, our logo, or the
wording for anything.
